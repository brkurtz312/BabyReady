What you'll learn

Eight modules, 44 lessons. Early Pregnancy Basics and every lesson on urgent warning signs are free.

  1. FIRST TRIMESTERFREE Early Pregnancy Basics First steps, common changes and when to reach out, and your first prenatal visit.
  2. MONTH BY MONTH How Your Baby Grows Your baby and your body, one trimester at a time.
  3. MODULE 1 Preparing for Birth Your third trimester, your care team and support team, birth preferences, and getting ready.
  4. MODULE 2 Labor Basics and When to Call How labor begins, when to call, the stages of labor, and birth.
  5. MODULE 3 Comfort, Movement, and Support Skills Breathing, positions, touch, and water, and how support people can help.
  6. MODULE 4 Interventions and Flexible Decisions Monitoring, pain relief, induction, assisted birth, cesarean birth, and when plans change.
  7. MODULE 5 Feeding and Newborn Care Feeding, safe sleep, car seats, infant CPR, and caring for your newborn.
  8. MODULE 6 Postpartum Recovery, Mood, and Red Flags Healing after birth, your emotional health, and the warning signs to know for you and your baby.
