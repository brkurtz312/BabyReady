# BabyReady Urgent Warning Signs Figure

## Purpose

Create a calm, high-contrast safety figure for the **First-Trimester Comfort & Care** lesson. It should help a learner recognize that certain symptoms need immediate medical attention without asking them to diagnose the cause.

This is a clinical-content draft for review. It is not learner-visible until it is reviewed, approved, published as media, and attached to the published Urgent Maternal Warning Signs safety card.

## Content strategy

### Primary learner action

Use one clear action for the entire graphic:

> **Get medical help right away.** Contact your obstetric care team. If you cannot reach them, go to the emergency room. If you think you are in immediate danger, call 911.

CDC advises seeking medical care immediately for an urgent maternal warning sign, and ACOG advises contacting a health care provider and getting help right away, with emergency-room care if the provider cannot be reached ([CDC urgent maternal warning signs](https://www.cdc.gov/hearher/maternal-warning-signs/index.html), [ACOG Patient Safety Resources](https://www.acog.org/giving/programs/quality-and-safety/resources)).

### First-trimester scope

This graphic is designed for pregnancy, particularly early pregnancy, and omits decreased fetal movement because that sign is better placed in a later-pregnancy learning module. The linked CDC source remains appropriate because it covers urgent warning signs during pregnancy; its postpartum-specific guidance should be reused in a dedicated postpartum module ([CDC urgent maternal warning signs](https://www.cdc.gov/hearher/maternal-warning-signs/index.html)).

## Figure storyboard

### Format and visual system

- **Asset type**: Static, portrait clinical figure.
- **Target size**: 4:5 portrait layout, legible when shown in the learner card and when enlarged in the image-only viewer.
- **Visual tone**: Calm, direct, non-alarmist. Warm off-white base, BabyReady teal for navigation and action, restrained cranberry-red only for the urgent action banner.
- **Reading order**: Header → action strip → four symptom groups → bottom reassurance and source line.
- **Graphic language**: Simple line icons and short labels. Do not use symptom photographs, blood imagery, or decorative illustrations.

## Exact learner-facing copy

### Header

**URGENT WARNING SIGNS**

**Get medical help right away during pregnancy.**

### Action banner

**Contact your obstetric care team now.**  
If you cannot reach them, go to the emergency room. If you think you are in immediate danger, call 911.

### Symptom group: breathing and chest

**BREATHING OR CHEST**

- Trouble breathing
- Chest pain or a fast-beating heart
- Fainting or passing out

### Symptom group: head and vision

**HEAD OR VISION**

- A headache that will not go away or gets worse
- Changes in your vision
- Dizziness or fainting

### Symptom group: pain, bleeding, and vomiting

**PAIN, BLEEDING, OR VOMITING**

- Severe belly pain that will not go away
- Bleeding that is more than spotting
- Fluid leaking from the vagina
- Severe nausea or vomiting, especially if you cannot keep fluids down

### Symptom group: fever and swelling

**FEVER OR SWELLING**

- Fever of **100.4°F (38°C) or higher**
- Extreme swelling of your face or hands
- Severe swelling, redness, or pain in one leg or arm

### Mental-health safety strip

**THOUGHTS OF HARMING YOURSELF OR YOUR BABY?**  
Get help now. You are not alone.

### Bottom reassurance

**If something feels seriously wrong, trust that concern and get help.**

Educational information only. It cannot diagnose a condition or replace care from your obstetric team.

## Clinical review notes

- **Linked primary source**: `cdc-urgent-maternal-warning-signs-2026`, already published in the BabyReady source library.
- **Supporting source**: ACOG Patient Safety Resources, which reinforces immediate action and emergency-room escalation when the care team cannot be reached ([ACOG Patient Safety Resources](https://www.acog.org/giving/programs/quality-and-safety/resources)).
- **Intentional simplification**: The figure groups related warning signs rather than reproducing the full CDC list. The linked learner safety card should retain the more complete explanatory text and source links.
- **Do not add**: Differential diagnoses, probability estimates, gestational-age-specific clinical thresholds beyond the CDC fever threshold, or a symptom-scoring tool.
- **Review decision requested**: Confirm that the wording “bleeding that is more than spotting” is appropriate for this early-pregnancy figure, given that the existing Bleeding During Pregnancy callout already advises learners to contact their obstetrician-gynecologist about any bleeding.

- Create the `clinicalMedia` record as a clinician-only draft.

- Submit the figure for clinical review before publication.
- After approval, create an immutable draft revision of `urgent-maternal-warning-signs-safety-card`, attach the published figure, complete review, and publish the revised card.
